ABOUT THE COACH

Bringing confidence to beginners — and building it from there.

I'm Christchurch-based, specialising in resistance training that targets hypertrophy, strength and fat loss. My focus is on bringing confidence to beginners — helping people build muscle, tone up, gain strength, and feel more confident in themselves.

I hold my qualification in Health & Fitness Personal Trainer (Level 4), and I'm currently studying my Advanced Health & Fitness Coach (Level 5). Everything I coach is built on proper education and real-world results — not guesswork.

All coaching is delivered 1:1 online, so you can train from your own gym on your schedule while still getting the accountability and guidance you need to progress.
